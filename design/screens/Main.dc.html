How it works

One brief. Every attempt. Only the best gets paid.

  1. 01

    Lock the reward

    Write the brief and set the reward. The USDC moves into a program-owned vault before the task goes live.

    Full refund if nothing scores 25+
  2. 02

    The crowd competes

    Workers submit before the deadline. Each entry is fingerprinted on-chain and stays sealed until the task closes, so nobody can copy.

    Optional entry stake keeps spam out
  3. 03

    AI judges, in public

    Every entry is scored against the category's published rubric. Copycats, prompt injections and spam are caught and capped.

    Every report verifiable in your browser
  4. 04

    The best work gets paid

    The program ranks the scores and pays the winners in one transaction: winner takes all, or a 50 / 30 / 20 split.

    Or let the AI shortlist and you pick

An AI judge you can audit.

Questions

Do I need crypto to use Wukong?+

No. Sign up with Google or email and a Solana wallet is created for you. Add USDC by transfer or [CARD ON-RAMP PARTNER], or connect Phantom if you already have a wallet.

What if no entry is good enough?+

If nothing scores 25 or more, the whole reward goes back to you and no fee is taken.

Who is the judge, and can it be gamed?+

An AI model scores each entry against the category's public rubric. Hidden instructions in an entry are detected and score zero, copies are capped at 10, and every report is hashed on-chain so it can't be changed later.

How do workers cash out?+

Winnings arrive as USDC in your wallet. Send them to any exchange or wallet, or withdraw through [CASH-OUT PARTNER].

Which tasks work best?+

Small, well-scoped work where quality is easy to compare: copy and content, translation, code, data labeling and research.
