Wukong, a work marketplace at wukonghq.xyz. You post a small, measurable task (copy with limits, a translation, a labeling set, a coding job with tests) and lock the reward, paid in USDC, before anyone starts.
Many people submit sealed entries. An AI judge scores every entry against a rubric published up front, and each score is public with a written reason.
Only the top entries are paid (winner takes all, or 50/30/20); the rest get a scored report. Wukong takes a 10% fee from the reward. A typical small task is a 50 USDC reward, so the all-in cost to you is about 55 USDC. If no entry clears the quality bar, you are refunded.
Organisations join by invitation, it is a new product with a small team, and it has not been independently audited. You would fund with USDC on Solana.
