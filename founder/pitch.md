Wukong, a work marketplace at wukonghq.xyz, built by Ansh, a security researcher. The code, the AI judge and its public benchmark (98 scored entries, 25 attack attempts) are open for anyone to read.
You post a small, measurable task (copy with limits, a translation, a labeling set, a coding job with tests). I set it up with you, write the scoring rubric from a ready-made template, and you approve it before anything goes live.
Many people submit sealed entries. An AI judge scores every entry against the rubric, and every entry, winner or not, gets a public score with a written reason. Only the top entries are paid. If no entry clears the quality bar you are refunded, and if you are unhappy with the top result I re-run the task once at no cost.
A typical small task is a 30 USDC reward, about 33 USDC all-in with a 10% fee, and your spend never goes above the reward you set. Until an independent audit is done, no reward is capped above 100 USDC.
Your first task is free: I fund a small reward (up to 30 USDC) myself. It is still USDC on Solana, organisations join by invitation, and the product has not been independently audited.
