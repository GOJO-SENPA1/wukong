# Buyer panel: Wukong (a competitive work marketplace where the buyer locks a reward and an AI judge scores the entries)

**5 buy · 35 pass** (12% buy) out of 40 simulated buyers. Seed 2026, so the same cards can be dealt again.

These are simulated buyers, not customers. Use this to find objections and weak spots, then confirm the big ones with real people before you spend.

## What would flip a no

- Named founders I can verify, a few real nonprofit or education customers I can contact, and a way to pay by card or invoice instead of USDC. The free trial task would help, but only once I can tell the company is real.
- A referral from someone I trust who has used it, plus an independent audit, and a way to pay by card so I don't have to handle USDC. The free first task would also have to cost me nothing in real money.
- A free trial where I see real entries judged against my rubric and the winning one clearly beats what Claude gives me, plus a way to pay by card and a reference from another organiser who used it.
- Let me pay by card or invoice in dollars, with a ready-made rubric template so I don't have to write one from scratch.
- Pay by card or invoice instead of USDC, plus a reference from another organisation like mine that has used it, with the free trial task run for me so there is no risk.
- An independent audit, plus a free first task where I can see the AI judge's scores and reasons on real entries and they beat what I get from Claude in ten minutes.
- Let me pay by card or invoice in dollars, with no wallet and no USDC on my side, plus a receipt I can hand to finance.
- A referral from another organiser I know who has used it, plus a human reviewing the winner before payout, a card or invoice payment option, and an independent audit.
- Let me pay by card or invoice with no wallet, and show me a few finished tasks where the AI judge's winner was clearly good. I'd still try the free task if the team really sets it up for me.
- Pay by card or invoice in dollars with no wallet on my side, plus a track record or an audit and fair pay for entrants, not only the winners.
- A free first task that shows a result clearly better than my freelancer or ChatGPT, plus a way to pay by card and an invoice I can expense.
- A free first task where I can see real scored entries and the judge's written reasons on a past task, with no crypto setup or money from me up front.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
